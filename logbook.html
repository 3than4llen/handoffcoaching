Questions

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise) and the current Claude desktop app on a Mac. On Team and Enterprise plans, your admin may need to allow plugins first. Windows is coming. Setup checks that your Mac has Python 3 and walks you through adding it if it doesn't.

Where does my business information go?

It stays in your own Claude account. The add-on itself collects nothing.

What if Claude skips a step?

At the end of every reply the add-on checks that Claude read your logbook and logged its work. If it didn't, it sends Claude back to finish.

Does it work when I use Claude on my phone?

It works in chats on your Mac today. I'm still testing chats started on the phone, and founders will hear from me as soon as that's confirmed.

Can I see what Claude keeps track of?

Anytime. It's a plain page in your own Claude account. Most days you won't need to open it.
