Why a UX designer teaches this

For thirty years my job was to make computers easier to use. That meant designing what you see on the screen: the menus, buttons and steps that turn what you want into something a computer can do. When the design is good, you don't notice it. When it's bad, you get stuck.

AI moves that work somewhere new. With Claude or ChatGPT you don't hunt through menus. You say what you want in your own words, and the assistant works out the steps. The screen matters less. What matters now is how the assistant is set up: what it knows about you and what it's allowed to touch.

That setup is the new user experience, and most people are building theirs by trial and error. I design it with you the way I'd design any product. We start with what you're trying to get done and how you work today, then build around that and test it together until it fits. It's the same process I used at TiVo and Best Buy. This time, the thing we're designing is how you work.

Questions

Which AI should I use: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot?

It depends on your work and the apps you already use. If your life runs on Google, Gemini fits right in; if it runs on Microsoft 365, Copilot might. Claude and ChatGPT are great all-rounders, and Perplexity is handy for research. On our free call I'll recommend one based on what you actually do, and I'm happy to set up more than one.

Is it safe to let AI work on my computer?

It can be, when it's set up carefully. We decide together which folders and apps it can reach, keep approvals on for anything important, and I'll show you how to review what it did. I never ask for your passwords. You type them yourself.

I'm not technical. Is this for me?

Yes. You don't need to be technical. If you can write an email, you can learn this. We go at your pace and you get written notes afterward.

Do I need to buy anything?

You'll likely want a paid plan of whichever assistant we pick (typically around $20/month for individuals), billed directly by the company that makes it. I'll tell you if the free tier is enough.

What if I need to reschedule?

No problem with 24 hours' notice. Just let me know.
