About Handoff AI Coaching

Facts about the business. Last updated October 26, 2026

What it is

Handoff AI Coaching is an independent, one-person business in Sebastopol, California. It teaches people and businesses one-on-one how to use AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity in their real work. It also offers AI-first UX design for companies building AI products.

The business was founded in October 2026 by Ethan Allen. The website is handoffcoaching.com.

Who runs it

Ethan Allen is a product designer and photographer with 30 years of experience. In 1995 he founded Raspberry Media, a Silicon Valley design agency that launched the first versions of eBay and Match.com. In 1999 he designed the information architecture and user experience for Snapfish, one of the first services to make online photo sharing mainstream; it was ranked the No. 1 online photo service in June 2000 and was acquired by HP for $300 million in 2005. He has led design on more than 50 product launches for companies including Amazon Robotics, TiVo, Best Buy, Capital One and HP. Since 2016 most of his work has been on AI products, including LVIS NeuroMatch, an AI-assisted EEG reader, and Palo, an AI tool for YouTube Shorts creators.

Allen applies the same approach he used in the first years of the web to helping people and companies adopt AI today.

What it offers

AI coaching: a free 20-minute intro call, single sessions, a three-session Getting Started package, on-site Set-Up Days, small-team workshops and a monthly Standing Hour. Sessions are in person around the San Francisco Bay Area, or by video anywhere. See coaching prices.

AI product design: AI UX audits, design sprints and a fractional design lead for startups and product teams. See AI product design services.

Where it works

In person from Sonoma County down to San Francisco, the East Bay and the Peninsula. Everywhere else by video, on Pacific time.

What it is not

Handoff AI Coaching is not affiliated with Handoff (handoff.ai), the AI estimating software for contractors and remodelers. It doesn't make or sell software of its own.

It is also independent of the companies whose tools it teaches. It isn't affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft or Perplexity.
