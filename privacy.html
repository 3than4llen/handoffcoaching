Privacy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Handoff is a one-person coaching business run by me, Ethan Allen, in California. This page explains what information this website collects and what I do with it. The short version: I only collect what you choose to send me, what I need to deliver what you buy, and basic visit counts, and I never sell any of it.

When you buy Handoff Logbook

Checkout for Handoff Logbook and Install with Ethan runs through Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy, which take the payment under their own privacy policies. I never see your card details. They send me your name and email so I can deliver what you bought, help you set it up and handle any refund. I don't use them for anything else unless you ask me to.

Handoff Logbook itself runs inside your own Claude account and doesn't send your business information to me or anyone else.

When you use the contact form

The form asks for your name and email, and optionally your phone number. It also includes whatever you write in your message. That information is delivered to my email inbox by Web3Forms, a form service, so I can reply to you. I use it only to answer your question and arrange sessions. I don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask me to.

Visit statistics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects details like your general location, device and browser type, and the pages you view. It doesn't tell me who you are. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.

If you'd rather not be counted, you can block cookies in your browser or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.

Who else sees your information

Only the services that make the site work: GitHub, which hosts it; Web3Forms, which delivers the form; Google, for the visit counts and the site's fonts; and Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy, for purchases. I don't sell or rent your information to anyone, and I don't share it for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Just email hello@handoffcoaching.com or send a note through the contact form and I'll take care of it within 30 days. Wherever you live, I'll honor these requests.

"Do Not Track" signals

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. There's no agreed standard for how sites should respond, so this site doesn't change its behavior when it receives one. The opt-out options above work regardless.

Children

This site isn't aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If anything on this page changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
