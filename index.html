Questions

Good questions people ask.

Claude or ChatGPT: which should I use?

It depends on your work and the apps you already use. Both are excellent. On our free call I'll recommend one based on what you actually do, and I'm happy to set up both.

Is it safe to let AI work on my computer?

It can be, when it's set up carefully. We decide together which folders and apps it can reach, keep approvals on for anything important, and I'll show you how to review what it did. I never ask for your passwords. You type them yourself.

I'm not technical. Is this for me?

Yes. Most of my clients aren't. If you can write an email, you can learn this. We go at your pace and you get written notes afterward.

Do I need to buy anything?

You'll likely want a paid plan of Claude or ChatGPT (typically around $20/month for individuals), billed directly by the provider. I'll tell you if the free tier is enough.

What if I need to reschedule?

No problem with 24 hours' notice. Life happens. Just let me know.

Why learn from you?

I've spent 30 years designing the screens millions of people use, including eBay's first interface and Match.com's launch. My whole job has been making new technology understandable. AI is the most powerful tool I've seen in that time, and I teach it the way I design: start with what you need, cut the jargon.
